

### **1. Mandatory Pre-Joining & Hiring Documents**

*   **Offer Letter (ऑफर लेटर)**
    *   **When:** Released after the final HR round.
    *   **Details:** Contains your Job Role, Salary Structure, Joining Date, and Employment Terms.
    *   **Responsibility:** You must acknowledge (accept) and sign this letter within a specific deadline (e.g., 12 or 24 hours) via email.
*   **Appointment Letter / Joining Letter (अपॉइंटमेंट लेटर)**
    *   **Details:** A formal agreement outlining your Job Responsibilities, Salary Structure, and Legal Terms.
    *   **Purpose:** Serves as the official formal agreement after you accept the offer.
*   **ID Proof & Address Proof (आईडी और एड्रेस प्रूफ)**
    *   **Documents:** Aadhar Card and PAN Card.
    *   **Purpose:** Mandatory for security verification, bank account creation, and tax filing (Form 16).
*   **Internship Letter (इंटर्नशिप लेटर)**
    *   **For:** Freshers or Students (usually those without prior experience).
    *   **Purpose:** Used to calculate the start date and prove 6 months of continuous duration for the company's records.

### **2. Optional / Conditional Documents**

*   **Previous Company Documents (प्रीवियस कंपनी के डॉक्यूमेंट्स)**
    *   **Includes:** Previous Offer Letter, Appointment Letter, and Service/Experience Letter.
    *   **Condition:** Only required if you are an *ex-employee*. If you are currently working, you generally do not provide these until you resign.
*   **Service Letter / Experience Letter (सर्विस लेटर / एक्सपीरियंस लेटर)**
    *   **For:** Existing employees.
    *   **Note:** Companies typically do not issue Experience Letters until full and final settlement is done.

### **3. Joining Day Documents (The "Joining Kit")**

*   **Employment Contract (एंप्लॉयमेंट कांट्रैक्ट)**
    *   **Details:** Includes job role details, terms of employment, company policies, leave structure, and probation period (usually 6 months).
    *   **Responsibility:** Must be read thoroughly and signed during the onboarding/induction session.
*   **Joining Kit Forms (जॉइनिंग किट)**
    *   **Includes:**
        *   **Personal Information Form:** Name, address, family background.
        *   **Bank Details Form:** Account number for salary credit.
        *   **Tax Related Forms:** Declaration for tax deduction (Old vs. New Regime).
        *   **PF Nomination Form:** Provident Fund nominee details.
        *   **Medical/Life Insurance Declaration:** Health insurance details.
    *   **Action:** These forms must be filled out and submitted physically or digitally on the day of joining.

### **4. Legal & Policy Documents**

*   **NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement) / गोपनी पॉलिसी**
    *   **Content:** Strict confidentiality regarding company data, client projects, and code.
    *   **Restriction:** You cannot share project details, take screenshots, or discuss company matters on public platforms/social media.
    *   **Consequence:** Legal action (court cases/blacklisting) if data is leaked (referenced in audio regarding AWS keys leak).
*   **Company Handbook / Policy Document (कंपनी पॉलिसी हैंडबुक)**
    *   **Format:** Often found on a company mobile app (e.g., "BeCognizant") rather than a physical book.
    *   **Contents:**
        *   Company Rules & Code of Conduct.
        *   **Leave Policy:** Details of Sick Leave (SL), Casual Leave (CL), Earned Leave (EL/Privilege Leave), and Maternity/Paternity leaves.
        *   Holidays: List of government holidays and firm-specific holidays.
    *   **HR Guidelines:** Rules regarding loans, advances, and health insurance.

### **5. Financial & Tax Documents**

*   **Form 16 (फॉर्म नंबर 16)**
    *   **Definition:** Annual statement showing Taxable Salary, Tax Deducted, and Tax Deposited.
    *   **Eligibility:** Only for employees paying tax (not usually for interns).
    *   **Issuance:** Automatically generated/provided by the company portal at the end of the financial year.
*   **Salary Slip (पेमेंट स्लिप)**
    *   **Contents:** Detailed breakdown including Allowances, HRA, Special Allowance, Deductions, and Tax.
    *   **Availability:** Provided monthly (can be downloaded from the portal).

### **6. IT & Security Access Documents**

*   **IT Assets Form (आईटी एसेट्स)**
    *   **Items:** Laptop, Mouse, Keyboard, Monitor, Charger, etc.
    *   **Process:** Items are issued with a "Custody Receipt" (कस्टडी रिसीट).
    *   **Access:** Creation of Official Email ID, Login credentials for internal tools (Jira, Confluence, Git), and VPN access.
*   **Security Protocols**
    *   **Rules:** Login allowed only via company laptop or authorized IP (no mobile login for work).
    *   **Monitoring:** Virtual Access cards track movement (entry/exit) to restricted areas.

### **7. Exit / Relieving Documents**

*   **Resignation Letter (रेजिग्नेशन लेटर)**
    *   **Process:** Formal email sent to HR and Reporting Manager.
    *   **Content:** Mentioning the Last Working Day (LWD) and serving the notice period.
    *   **Action:** Triggers the clearance process (IT asset return, library clearance, etc.).
*   **Relieving Letter (रिलीविंग लेटर)**
    *   **Details:** States that you have been relieved from your duties as of a specific date.
    *   **Use:** Required as proof for the next company to show that you have successfully left the previous job.
*   **Experience Letter (एक्सपीरियंस लेटर)**
    *   **Details:** Summarizes your total work experience, tenure, and role during your employment.
    *   **Issue:** Usually provided upon full and final settlement.
