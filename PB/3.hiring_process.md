
# Hiring Process Workflow

### Step 1: Initial Preparation
Before applying, ensure the profile matches the specific firm type (Government vs. MNC) to avoid shortlisting issues.
*   **Action:** Prepare **three types** of resumes.
*   **Awareness:** Understand which documents are accepted or restricted by Government firms and MNCs (especially those with strict background verification).

### Step 2: Recruitment Rounds
Complete the technical and interview rounds conducted by the company.
*   **Final Round (HR Round):**
    *   Verbal discussion regarding the job role.
    *   Discussion on joining dates.
    *   Discussion on salary and company norms.

### Step 3: Offer Letter Release & Acceptance
After the HR discussion, the company formally releases the Offer Letter.
*   **Offer Letter Contents:** Job role, salary structure, joining date, and employment terms.
*   **Candidate Action:**
    1.  **Acknowledge:** Formally accept the offer (usually required within 12-24 hours).
    2.  **E-Sign:** Digitally sign the offer letter.
    3.  **Revert:** Email the signed copy back to the HR.

### Step 4: Document Submission
Once the offer is acknowledged, submit the required documents for verification.
*   **Mandatory Documents:**
    *   Previous Company Offer Letter.
    *   Payment Slips & Bank Statement (Minimum 3 months, up to 6 months).
*   **Conditional/Optional Documents:**
    *   Previous Joining/Appointment Letter (if asked).
    *   Experience/Service Letter (Only for ex-employees; *not* for current employees/interns).
    *   Internship Letter (Required for interns).
    *   Certificates (10th marks, course completion certificates).
    *   Passbook (Optional).

### Step 5: Appointment Letter Issuance
The timing of this letter depends on the work mode.
*   **Scenario A (Work From Home):**
    *   Appointment Letter is provided *before* the joining date (after offer acceptance).
*   **Scenario B (Work From Office):**
    *   Appointment Letter is provided *on the day of joining* during the induction process.
*   **Action:** Sign and retain a copy.

### Step 6: Induction & Orientation
On the joining date, attend the induction session to understand the organization.
*   **HR Session:** Introduction to company hierarchy (Founders, CTOs, Team Leads), culture, and environment.
*   **Policy Overview:** Explanation of POSH Act, legal terms, and company policies.

### Step 7: Legal Agreements (NDA & Contracts)
Sign critical legal documents to ensure compliance and data security.
*   **Employment Contract:** Details terms of employment, confidentiality clauses, and company policies.
    *   *Note:* Some companies may have separate Bond/Deposit policies (12-24 months) requiring security checks.
*   **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA):**
    *   Strict agreement to protect sensitive data (client names, project architecture, credentials like AWS Keys).
    *   **Warning:** Breach (e.g., sharing screens on social media) leads to immediate termination/blacklisting.

### Step 8: Joining Kit & Policy Handbook
Complete the final administrative formalities to activate employee status.
*   **Joining Kit Forms:** Fill out forms for:
    *   Personal Information (Address, Family background).
    *   Bank Details (Salary account).
    *   Tax Forms (Annual tax declarations).
*   **Company Policy Handbook (or HRMS App):** Access details regarding:
    *   Code of Conduct.
    *   Leave Policies (Casual, Sick, Paid).
    *   Holiday Calendar (Depends on company origin: India vs. Foreign).
    *   Health Insurance & Loan policies.
